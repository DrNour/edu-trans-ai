About

AI that strengthens human judgement.

EduTransAI is the research and innovation platform of Dr Noureldin Abdelaal, Associate Professor at the United Arab Emirates University.

Its work develops and evaluates tools for translator education, machine-translation post-editing, bilingual quality assessment, and classroom analytics. AI can support analysis, feedback, and learning; informed human judgement remains essential.

Research focus

Evidence-led multilingual AI

EduTransAI investigates how Arabic–English AI can be useful, rigorous, and culturally responsible in real educational and professional contexts.

  • Machine-translation post-editing and quality evaluation
  • AI literacy and assessment in translator education
  • Arabic NLP and bilingual text analytics
  • Learning analytics and evidence-informed teaching
  • Responsible use of generative AI in higher education
Collaboration

Research, workshops, and partnership.

EduTransAI welcomes collaboration with universities, technology researchers, translation organisations, and public-sector partners.

Current areas include translator education, post-editing analytics, Arabic–English evaluation datasets, AI literacy, multilingual NLP, and evidence-based institutional AI policy.
